About SticKeys

SticKeys is a high-energy mashup band blending Western hits with Eastern musical textures. With professional musicianship and powerful stage presence, the band creates unforgettable live experiences for corporate events, weddings, hotels, and large-scale performances.

Amr Galal

Drummer • Band Lead

With over 20 years of professional experience, Amr has performed alongside top artists and delivered high-impact live performances. His powerful rhythm and stage leadership define the energy of SticKeys.

Heba Solieman

Pianist • Keyboardist • Composer

Heba brings a refined musical depth to SticKeys with her background in theory and composition. Her performance style blends technical excellence with emotional connection on stage.
